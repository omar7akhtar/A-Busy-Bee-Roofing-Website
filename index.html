About Us

Local Roofing Backed by Experience

Ben, The Original Bee. Known across South County for honest work, dependable roofing, and getting the job done right.

At Busy Bee Roofing, we’ve proudly served Southern Rhode Island and the South County area since 1986 with dependable roofing solutions built on honesty, hard work, and quality craftsmanship. Based out of South Kingstown, our team specializes in residential and commercial roofing services including roof installation, roof replacement, roof repair and maintenance, emergency roofing services, roof inspections, storm damage repair, and gutter installation and repair.
