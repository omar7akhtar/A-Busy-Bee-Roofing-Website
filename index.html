About Us

Local Roofing Backed by Experience

At Busy Bee Roofing, we’ve proudly served Southern Rhode Island and the South County area since 1986 with dependable roofing solutions built on honesty, hard work, and quality craftsmanship. Based out of South Kingstown, our team specializes in residential and commercial roofing services including roof installation, roof replacement, roof repair and maintenance, emergency roofing services, roof inspections, storm damage repair, and gutter installation and repair. For nearly four decades, homeowners and businesses throughout the area have trusted us for reliable service, fast response times, and roofing work that’s built to withstand New England weather. Whether it’s a small repair or a full roof replacement, we take pride in treating every property like it’s our own.

Ben and his team bring a neighborly, hands-on approach to every job, combining local knowledge with the professionalism customers expect from an insured roofing company.
